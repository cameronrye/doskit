DOSBox and DOSBox-X, from emulators 8.3.8

The files the player loads from /emulators/: emulators.js, and DOSBox (wdosbox.js, wdosbox.wasm), DOSBox-X (wdosbox-x.js, wdosbox-x.wasm) and libzip (wlibzip.js, wlibzip.wasm) compiled to WebAssembly. They come from emulators, js-dos's emulator package by Alexander Guryanov (caiiiycuk), under the GPL-2.0.
